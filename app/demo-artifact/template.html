The source PowerPoint keys this as five steps 200ms apart at ±2°, which is invisible on a phone. The motion spec keeps the rhythm and amplifies it to ±7°. Driving a spring at the client's own stiffness lands the turning points on roughly that 200ms cadence on its own — the impulse is solved backwards from the target angle, because damping bleeds off about a fifth of the first swing.

Pivot is re-homed at the base in code rather than in a modelling tool, so the chair turns about its column instead of its own middle. Taps go through an invisible box collider — a generous target for a small thumb, and twelve triangles to hit-test instead of twenty-four thousand.
