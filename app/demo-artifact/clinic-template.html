What is 3D here

Only the chair. The room is a picture — 111 KB and no geometry at all — because nothing in it moves. The chair is real geometry, so it can teeter, glow and be walked around.

The teeter

A damped spring, not keyframes. The storyboard keys five beats 200ms apart at ±2°; this reaches ±7° and lands the beats on that cadence by physics. Tap twice quickly and the swing builds.

Where the words live

Every word is HTML above the canvas, never drawn inside it — so it stays selectable, translatable and readable by a screen reader. The voice is the same recording already in the game.
